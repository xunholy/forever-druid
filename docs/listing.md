# Listing text (CurseForge / Wago)

**Name:** Forever Druid (the addon folder stays `ForeverDruid`)

**Summary:** A druid HUD built for WoW Forever: mana while shapeshifted, energy and rage, combo
points, cooldowns for the form you're in, your DoTs and HoTs on target, and click-to-cast buff
reminders.

**Description**

Forever Druid puts everything a druid needs on Forever in one column under your character, and
the column changes with your form.

- **Cat Form:** mana bar with the cost of shifting back marked, an energy bar, combo points that turn red at five, and Tiger's Fury, Dash and Prowl.
- **Bear Form:** Enrage, Bash, Frenzied Regeneration, Feral Charge and Barkskin.
- **Caster form:** Nature's Swiftness, Innervate, Rebirth and your healing or Balance cooldowns,
  depending on where your talent points are.
- **Your effects on your target:** Rake, Rip, Moonfire, Insect Swarm, Faerie Fire and Lacerate on
  enemies; Rejuvenation and Regrowth on friends.
- **Reminders** for Mark of the Wild, Thorns and low reagents. Click a reminder to cast it.
- **Skyborne:** Read Leyline and Walk on Air on the cooldown row, and a reminder when you're not
  attuned to a ley line.

Cooldowns flash when they're back, dim when you can't use them, turn blue when you're short on
mana, rage or energy, and red when your target is out of range. All of it keeps working in combat
on Forever's client, which hides most numbers from addons during a fight.

Arrange it from the minimap icon: unlock, drag the whole column or shift-drag one piece out, nudge
with the arrow keys, turn parts on and off, change size and fading. Profiles can be shared as a
text string.

English, German, French and Spanish.
